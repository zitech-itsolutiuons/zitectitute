# Design system

## Direction

**The Annotated Course Reader**: an editorial learning workspace inspired by well-used academic readers, with visible lecture marks and useful study annotations. Zitectitute should feel calm enough for long reading sessions and precise enough to support instructor operations.

## Foundations

- **Palette:** warm paper and soft ink for the reading environment; evergreen for primary actions and confirmed states; muted ochre for selected lectures, annotations, and small navigational emphasis. Dark mode uses deep green-black surfaces and warm paper text.
- **Typography:** Source Sans 3 for controls, labels, and dense data; Source Serif 4 for page titles and learning content. Use tabular figures for progress and analytics.
- **Materials:** quiet paper surfaces, restrained rules, and small margin-like markers. Avoid decorative gradients, glass, and stacked card grids.
- **Composition:** clear page titles, generous reading measure, course lists with visible progress, and compact data tables where instructors need them.
- **Interaction:** selected and completed states are explicit; lecture navigation keeps progress close to the reading outline and preserves nested subsection navigation; controls retain clear keyboard focus and comfortable touch targets.

## First view and signature interaction

The public homepage speaks directly to people learning web design and programming. A small HTML-to-browser example gives the subject its own visual signature, followed by a clear index of web design, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, and computer-science learning areas and the actual published course catalog. Within a course, the lecture rail acts like an annotated table of contents: learners can expand subsections, see reading and quiz progress, and return to their place without losing the lesson.

## Cross-surface reach

Use the same paper-and-ink foundation across public, account, learner, instructor, and administrator routes. Give dense instructor tables more compact spacing without changing the underlying navigation language. Preserve dark mode, responsive layouts, existing content, and all current application workflows.

## Product truth

Zitectitute serves instructors and learners across courses. Do not present institution-specific homepage defaults, sample material, learner counts, or outcome claims as universal product facts.
